Breath of Fire III – 32,767 Damage Limit

This patch raises Breath of Fire III's 9,999 damage limit to 32,767. Damage is
still calculated normally: low-damage attacks are unchanged, and only results
that exceed the original limit are affected. The game can display five-digit
damage correctly, so attacks above 9,999 now show their actual value.

The patch is for the USA v1.1 PlayStation release, SLUS-00422. It is applied to
a raw 2,352-byte-sector Track 1 BIN. The included Python patcher checks the
source SHA-256, creates a separate output file, patches both copies of the
battle overlay, and regenerates the modified sectors' EDC/ECC data.

The limit is 32,767 because battle damage is stored as a signed 16-bit value.
Going higher would cause values to become negative and would require a larger
rewrite of the battle result, HP, and display code.

No game files are included. A legally obtained matching disc image is required.
