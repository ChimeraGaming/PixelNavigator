# Breath of Fire GBA map catalog

All 496 USA ABFE revision 0 map IDs are listed. Names come from the cartridge menu. The game leaves 45 entries unnamed, including the world map and cutscenes. Descriptive labels for cutscene artwork identify what was seen; they do not mean the scene is unused. Different IDs can share a native location name. No floor or story-state names have been guessed.

The purple face from the Flying Omelette screenshot is map 103 at X 632, Y 28796. Map 102 displays a muted palette variant. These are cutscene assets, not confirmed unused playable areas.

Entry coordinates come from native transitions or spawn records unless marked as visually checked. Those records are not a guarantee that an unfinished area is safe to explore.
